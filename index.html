Your panel van, turned into a camper

Camper conversions in Buxton, Derbyshire, built to order on the van you already own. John specialises in VW Transporters from the T5 to the T7, and takes on other makes and sizes too.

What John does

Full Conversions

Everything it takes to turn an empty panel van into a camper: stripping out, insulation and lining, windows, electrics, furniture, a kitchen and a pop-top roof. About four weeks of work for a medium-wheelbase van.

Upgrades

Any single part of a Conversion done on its own, such as a pop-top roof, side windows or leisure electrics, for vans that are already part way there.

Free Inspection and Quote

Bring your van to the Buxton workshop and John will look it over for free, then give you an individual Quote and a start date.

How a Conversion works

Built by John

John spent his career in manufacturing. When he retired, he put those skills into converting vans, and he has converted six since 2023.

He does every job himself and won't call a van finished until it's right. If a problem with his work turns up in the two months after Collection, he'll repair it.
