# JR Camper Conversions

The public website for JR Camper Conversions, a workshop in Buxton, Derbyshire, that converts Customers' own panel vans into camper vans to order.

## Language

### The work

**Conversion**:
The full job of turning a Customer's panel van into a camper van, done to order.
_Avoid_: Sale, camper sale, product, full build

**Upgrade**:
A single piece of work that would normally form part of a Conversion (e.g. a pop-top roof, windows, electrics), done on its own.
_Avoid_: Job, add-on, extra, service

**Build**:
The documented record of one Conversion, from the van as supplied to the finished camper, shown on the site as evidence of JR's work.
_Avoid_: Project, gallery, portfolio item

**Stage**:
One named step within a Build (e.g. stripped out, roof lifted, wiring), shown as a group of captioned photos. Builds are full Conversions only; Upgrade work never forms a Build.
_Avoid_: Step, phase, section

### The people

**JR**:
Short for JR Camper Conversions, the business. JR is not a person.
_Avoid_: Using "JR" to mean John

**John**:
The person who runs JR and does the Conversions and Upgrades himself.
_Avoid_: JR, the owner, the team, "we"

**Customer**:
A person who commissions a Conversion or Upgrade from JR, always on a van they already own.
_Avoid_: Buyer, client

### From Enquiry to Collection

In order: Enquiry → Inspection → Quote → Deposit → the work → Collection → Final payment → Aftercare.

**Enquiry**:
A message from a prospective Customer asking about a Conversion or Upgrade, sent to JR's inbox.
_Avoid_: Lead, contact, submission

**Inspection**:
John's free look over the Customer's van at the Buxton workshop, before giving a Quote.
_Avoid_: Survey, assessment, viewing

**Quote**:
JR's individual price for a specific Conversion or Upgrade, given after the Inspection. There are no fixed or "from" prices.
_Avoid_: Price list, estimate, pricing

**Deposit**:
Half of the Quote, paid by the Customer to book the work.
_Avoid_: Down payment, upfront payment, booking fee

**Collection**:
The Customer picking up their finished van from the workshop.
_Avoid_: Handover, delivery, purchase

**Final payment**:
The remaining half of the Quote, paid at Collection.
_Avoid_: Balance, remainder

**Aftercare**:
John's promise to repair problems with a Conversion or Upgrade that the Customer didn't cause, for two months after Collection. It is on top of the Customer's legal rights, not instead of them.
_Avoid_: Warranty, guarantee
