# JR Camper Conversions

The public website for JR Camper Conversions, a workshop in Buxton, Derbyshire, that converts Customers' own panel vans into camper vans to order.

## Language

### The work

**Conversion**:
The full job of turning a Customer's panel van into a camper van, done to order.
_Avoid_: Sale, camper sale, product, full build

**Upgrade**:
A single piece of work that would normally form part of a Conversion (e.g. a pop-top roof, windows, electrics), done on its own.
_Avoid_: Job, add-on, extra, service

**Build**:
The documented record of one Conversion, from the van as supplied to the finished camper, shown on the site as evidence of JR's work.
_Avoid_: Project, gallery, portfolio item

**Stage**:
One named step within a Build (e.g. stripped out, roof lifted, wiring), shown as a group of captioned photos. Builds are full Conversions only; Upgrade work never forms a Build.
_Avoid_: Step, phase, section

### The people

**Customer**:
A person who commissions a Conversion or Upgrade from JR, always on a van they already own.
_Avoid_: Buyer, client

**Enquiry**:
A message from a prospective Customer asking about a Conversion or Upgrade, sent to JR's inbox.
_Avoid_: Lead, contact, submission

**Quote**:
JR's individual price for a specific Conversion or Upgrade, given in reply to an Enquiry. There are no fixed or "from" prices.
_Avoid_: Price list, estimate, pricing
