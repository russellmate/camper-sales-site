White Transporter T6 with pop-top roof

A plain white panel van turned into a pop-top camper with a full kitchen, electrics, side windows and new wheels.

Base van: Volkswagen Transporter T6

Draft: The description of each stage is a placeholder until John approves the final copy.

How it was built

  1. 1

    As supplied

    The Customer's van arrived as a standard white panel van with steel wheels and no side windows.

  2. 2

    Stripped out

    The load area was cleared back to bare metal, ready for insulation, lining and the floor.

  3. 3

    Electrics

    A leisure electrical system was wired in to run the lights, fridge and sockets separately from the van's own battery.

  4. 4

    Furniture and kitchen

    Dark grey furniture units were built along the side wall, with a worktop, cupboards, a control panel and a built-in fridge.

  5. 5

    Pop-top roof

    A pop-top roof was fitted, giving standing room in camp and space for an upstairs bed.

  6. 6

    Finished

    The finished camper, with tinted windows, black alloy wheels and a rear spoiler.
